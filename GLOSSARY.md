# andresbetov portfolio

Portfolio site for Andrés Bermúdez. The page presents selected work, professional experience, and education as a sequence of sections.

## Language

**Section**:
A top-level band of the portfolio with its own opener, content, and anchor.
_Avoid_: block, panel

**Hero**:
The opening section that introduces Andrés and presents the primary actions.
_Avoid_: landing, banner

**Featured project**:
A long-form section for one selected project, combining narrative, evidence, and diagrams.
_Avoid_: case study, project card

**Shell**:
The shared layout and visual foundation used by every section: background, grid, navigation, tokens, and typography.
_Avoid_: layout wrapper, chrome

**Design token**:
A named color, type, spacing, or radius value from the Figma Foundations.
_Avoid_: style variable, constant

**Engineering Discipline orbit**:
The circular diagram in the Hero with the capability badges around its center label.
_Avoid_: orbit graphic, rings

**CV**:
The downloadable PDF linked from the Hero and Footer.
_Avoid_: resume
